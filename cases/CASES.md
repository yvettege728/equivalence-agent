# Real substitutions, recorded by the person

Four cases from one move, London to Boston. Images in `cases/images/`.
These are the ground truth for what "substitute" means to this person. Read
them before proposing anything, and say which case a new item resembles.

## 1. Cookie: a jump across categories

- Lost: M&S pistachio cookies (`pistachio_cookie_uk.avif`). M&S has no US stores.
- Chosen substitute: Trader Joe's Dark Chocolate Bark with puffed quinoa and
  dried raspberries (`darkchoco_us.webp`).
- Ritual layer: use, of the reward kind. Triggered by being tired or by finishing
  a deadline, not by a time of day.
- What was preserved: the occasion and the act of treating oneself.
- What was dropped: category, material, brand, packaging. A biscuit became a
  chocolate bark.

**Lesson: when the ritual is a reward, the substitute has to hold the occasion,
not the product. Material and sign similarity are almost irrelevant here.**

## 2. Granola: the person assembled the substitute

- Lost: a regular granola (`granola_uk.avif`).
- Chosen substitute: mixing her own at the Whole Foods bulk dispensers
  (`granola_us.jpg`, `granolo_us.webp`).
- Ritual layer: use, of the periodic kind, plus a new element of composition.
- What was preserved: the material result, roughly.
- What was added: authorship. The substitute is not a product on a shelf, it is
  something she makes.

**Lesson: a substitute can be a practice rather than a product. When no single
item matches, composition is an option, and it can end up preferred.**

## 3. Oat milk: the brand survived but was not recognised

- Used to buy: Califia Farms Oat Barista Blend in the UK, a 1 litre carton
  (`oatmilk_uk.jpg`).
- The same brand in the US sells oat milk mainly in a tall curved bottle
  (`oatmilk_us.webp`), a different pack format and a different look.
- What happened: she did not find the brand, so she switched to Chobani Oatmilk
  Extra Creamy (`oatmilk_us_2.webp`) and stayed there.
- Ritual layer: use, periodic. Property layer that failed: sign.

**Lesson: a sign-layer failure can cost a brand its customer even when the
material and the brand are both available. Recognition is a precondition for
loyalty, not a consequence of it. An agent that matches on brand name would have
solved this instantly; the person, matching on appearance, could not.**

## 4. Soya milk

- `soya_milk_uk.webp` is on file. The person has not yet said what happened to

## Three mechanisms, so far

| Mechanism | Case | What the agent should look for |
|---|---|---|
| Category jump | cookie | the occasion, not the product |
| Self-assembly | granola | whether components exist, and whether she would enjoy composing |
| Unintended switch | oat milk | whether the original is actually here under a different look |

An agent's first job on a new item is to say which of these three is in play,
and to check mechanism 3 before accepting any switch the person already made.
